Fort Collins Lakes

Swimming, Boating & Beach Days

Fort Collins has some of Colorado's best lake recreation. From the iconic Horsetooth Reservoir to quieter swimming holes, here's where to get on the water.

More: Horsetooth Guide | Fort Collins Guide

Horsetooth Reservoir

Fort Collins' Crown Jewel

6.5 miles of Rocky Mountain-backed water. Swimming beaches, boat rentals, cliff jumping, and stunning sunsets.

Boyd Lake State Park

Family-Friendly Beach

Sandy swim beach, calmer waters for paddleboarding, and great camping. Easier parking than Horsetooth.
