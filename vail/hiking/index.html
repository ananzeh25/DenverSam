Vail Hiking

Summer Adventures on the Mountain

When the snow melts, Vail transforms into a hiker's paradise. Wildflower-filled meadows, alpine lakes, and stunning peaks. Here are the best summer hikes in Vail.

More: Vail Restaurants | Estes Park Hiking

Booth Falls

4.4 miles • Moderate

Vail's most popular hike. A 60-foot waterfall through aspen groves. Best in early summer when the falls are roaring.

Piney River Falls

6 miles • Easy

Flat trail along Piney Lake to a beautiful waterfall. Great for families. Stunning fall colors in September.
