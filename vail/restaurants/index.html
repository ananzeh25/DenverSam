Vail Restaurants

Mountain Dining at Its Finest

Vail's dining scene rivals its skiing. From Michelin-worthy fine dining in Vail Village to hearty apres-ski spots in Lionshead, the mountain delivers culinary experiences as memorable as the powder. Whether you're fueling up for first tracks or celebrating a bluebird day, here's where to eat.

Planning a ski trip? Check our Vail summer hiking guide or explore Breckenridge restaurants for more Summit County dining.
