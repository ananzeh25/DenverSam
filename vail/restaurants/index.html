Vail Restaurants

Where mountain culture meets culinary excellence

Vail isn't just a ski town—it's become one of Colorado's premier dining destinations. From white-tablecloth fine dining to casual spots where ski boots are welcome, this guide covers the restaurants worth your time and money with all the details you need to book a table.

Practical Tips for Dining in Vail

  • Reservations are essential — During ski season (December-March), book fine dining spots 2-4 weeks ahead.
  • Prices are high — Budget $75-150 per person for fine dining, $30-50 for casual dinner.
  • Parking is tricky — Use the Vail Village or Lionshead parking structures ($30/day) or take the free bus.
  • Dress code varies — Fine dining expects resort casual. Casual spots welcome ski clothes.
