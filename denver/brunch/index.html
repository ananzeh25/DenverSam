Denver Brunch

Weekend mornings done right

Denver takes brunch seriously. Maybe it's the late weekend sunrise, the recovery from Friday's brewery hopping, or the fact that Colorado's best activities happen after 10am anyway—whatever the reason, the city has developed one of the best brunch scenes in the country. From classic greasy spoons to chef-driven breakfast experiences, you'll find something for every craving and budget.

This guide covers spots that locals actually frequent—places where the food justifies the wait, the drinks are properly made, and the atmosphere makes lingering feel natural. Skip the tourist traps and eat where Denver eats.

Brunch Tips from Locals

  • Go early or late — Peak brunch is 10am-noon. Arrive at 9am or after 1pm to skip the worst waits.
  • Weekdays win — Most brunch spots serve Tuesday-Sunday. Tuesday brunch has no wait and the same food.
  • Check apps — Many spots use Yelp Waitlist or Nowait. Put your name in from home.
  • Bottomless deals vary — Some are $20 mimosas for 2 hours, some are $30 for unlimited everything. Ask what's included.
  • Tipping is standard — 18-20% on full check including drinks. Brunch service is demanding.
