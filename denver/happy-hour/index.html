Denver Happy Hour

The best deals in the Mile High City

Denver happy hour is a competitive sport. The city's density of restaurants and bars means they have to work to earn your 4-6pm business—and they do. Half-price appetizers, $5 craft cocktails, $3 beers: the deals are real and widespread. This guide cuts through the noise to highlight the spots where happy hour is genuinely worth your time, not just technically exists.

Times and specials change, so always verify before showing up—but these spots have consistently delivered value over time.

Downtown & LoDo

Tamayo

Larimer Square

Upscale Mexican with a rooftop patio and one of downtown's best happy hours. The margaritas are discounted, the tacos are half-price, and the guacamole is made tableside. The rooftop fills up fast at 4pm—arrive early to snag a table with views. One of the few fine-dining-quality spots with genuinely good happy hour value.

Hours: 3-6pm daily, 3-close Sunday

$$ at HH Rooftop

Guard and Grace

17th Street

One of Denver's best steakhouses with a surprisingly accessible happy hour. The bar area offers discounted drinks and a menu of smaller plates—wagyu sliders, oysters, carpaccio—at prices that won't break you. It's a way to experience a $200 dinner spot for $50. The cocktails are excellent even at regular price.

Hours: 4-6pm weekdays, bar area only

$$$ Upscale

Izakaya Den

South Pearl (also downtown-ish)

The excellent Japanese izakaya from the Sushi Den team offers great happy hour value. Half-price sushi, discounted sake, and small plates that showcase the kitchen's skill—all in a stylish setting. The quality matches the full-price menu; you're just getting it for less. One of the best high-end happy hours in Denver.

Hours: 4:30-6pm Tuesday-Saturday

$$ Japanese

RiNo & LoHi

Work & Class

RiNo

Wood-fired American food in RiNo with excellent drinks and a lively atmosphere. The happy hour features discounted cocktails (they're known for their whiskey program) and select small plates. The vibe is casual but the food is serious. The outdoor space is great in warm weather. Consistently packed for good reason.

Hours: 4-6pm weekdays

$$ Wood-Fired

Linger

LoHi

The converted mortuary turned global comfort food restaurant has an extensive happy hour menu. Discounted versions of their international small plates, cheap drinks, and access to that incredible rooftop. It's a scene, especially in summer—come for the deals, stay for the views and people-watching. One of the most fun happy hours in town.

Hours: 4-6:30pm daily

$$ Rooftop

Neighborhood Gems

Ace Eat Serve (Uptown) — Ping pong, Asian street food, and a fun happy hour. $5 sake bombs, cheap beer, discounted food. It's a party.

Cholon (LoDo) — Modern Asian with a sophisticated happy hour. Half-off dim sum and discounted cocktails. The French onion soup dumplings are essential.

Tag (Larimer Square) — Continental comfort food with excellent drink deals. The burger at happy hour price is one of the city's best values.

Mercantile (Union Station) — Farm-to-table in a beautiful space. The bar menu happy hour offers quality at manageable prices.

Happy Hour Tips

  • Arrive at 4pm — Most happy hours start between 3-5pm. Early gets you a seat; late gets you a wait.
  • Bar seating usually required — Many places limit happy hour to bar and patio, not tables.
  • Weekdays are best — Some spots skip happy hour on weekends. Check before going.
  • Tuesday is the move — Many restaurants run extended or enhanced happy hours on slow Tuesday nights.
  • Apps change — The Denver Happy Hour app (and similar) can help track current deals.
