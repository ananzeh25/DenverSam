Denver Date Night

Impress without the stress

Planning a date in Denver? Whether it's a first date, anniversary, or just breaking out of the dinner-and-movie routine, the city delivers. From candlelit fine dining to creative experiences that give you something to talk about, these picks have been tested for that crucial combination: impressive atmosphere, excellent food, and the kind of vibe that makes conversation flow.

Romantic Fine Dining

When you want the full experience: white tablecloths, tasting menus, and the kind of evening you'll remember.

Beckon

RiNo • Tasting Menu

An intimate 24-seat restaurant serving a multi-course tasting menu. Every detail is considered—the plating, the pacing, the way each course builds on the last. It's the kind of dinner where you lose track of time. Not for every date (too intense for a first date), but perfect for celebrating something or simply committing to an experience together. Book well ahead.

Reserve: Weeks in advance. It's worth it.

$$$$ Special Occasion

Tavernetta

Union Station • Italian

Sophisticated Italian in one of Denver's most beautiful dining rooms. The space—soaring ceilings, warm lighting, open kitchen—sets the mood immediately. The pasta is handmade and exceptional. The wine list is deep. It's upscale without being stuffy, and the energy is just right for conversation. Walking through Union Station after adds to the evening.

Perfect for: When you want impressive but not intimidating.

$$$ Beautiful Room

Frasca Food and Wine

Boulder (worth the drive)

Colorado's most celebrated restaurant, focusing on the cuisine of Friuli-Venezia Giulia. The tasting menus are exquisite, the wine pairings exceptional, and the service is warm rather than formal. It's in Boulder, but the 40-minute drive adds to the event feel. A genuine special-occasion destination that delivers on every level.

Note: Book 2-4 weeks ahead. Worth the planning.

$$$$ Destination

Atmosphere Without the Price Tag

Romantic doesn't have to mean expensive. These spots deliver great vibes at reasonable prices.

Williams & Graham

LoHi • Speakeasy

A speakeasy hidden behind a bookcase. The entry alone is a conversation starter—through a vintage bookshop, past a sliding shelf, into a candlelit bar with serious cocktails. The drinks are crafted by people who care, and the atmosphere is intimate without being cramped. Perfect for a first or fifth date. No reservations; arrive early or expect to wait.

Tip: Go Tuesday-Thursday to minimize the wait.

$$ Speakeasy

Cart-Driver

RiNo • Pizza & Oysters

Wood-fired pizza and oysters in a converted shipping container. The combination sounds odd but works beautifully. Share a pizza, order oysters, drink good wine, and enjoy the creative setting. It's casual enough for a first date but interesting enough to spark conversation. The outdoor seating is especially good in warm weather.

Order: Oysters, burrata pizza, and a bottle of wine.

$$ Unique

Creative Date Ideas

Beyond dinner: experiences that give you something to do together.

Ice Cream + Walking — Sweet Action or Little Man Ice Cream, then a stroll through the neighborhood. Low pressure, good vibes.

Brewery Hop — Pick a neighborhood (RiNo, LoDo) and hit 2-3 spots. Casual, plenty to talk about.

Jazz at Dazzle — Dinner and live jazz in a classic supper club setting. More character than a regular restaurant.

Cooking Class — Sur La Table, Stir Cooking School, or others offer couples classes. Active, collaborative, and you eat what you make.

Rooftop Sunset — 54thirty or Linger rooftop. Time it right, get there 30 minutes early, watch the mountains light up.
