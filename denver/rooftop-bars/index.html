Denver Rooftop Bars

Skyline Views & Sunset Cocktails

With 300 days of sunshine and those mountain views, Denver was made for rooftop drinking. From sleek hotel lounges to casual beer gardens, here are the best rooftop bars in the Mile High City.

Looking for more? Check out our Denver happy hour guide or Denver breweries.

Best Rooftop Bars

54thirty

Denver's Highest Rooftop Bar

Atop Le Méridien hotel, 54thirty offers unobstructed 360-degree views of the Rockies and downtown. Craft cocktails, fire pits, and the best sunsets in town.

$$$ Downtown

Death & Co

NYC Cocktail Legends in Denver

The Ramble Hotel's crown jewel. World-class cocktails on a stunning rooftop. Reservations recommended for this RiNo hotspot.

$$$ RiNo

ViewHouse

Sports, Views & Good Times

Massive rooftop with games, multiple bars, and mountain views. Great for groups. Locations in Ballpark and Centennial.

$$ Ballpark
