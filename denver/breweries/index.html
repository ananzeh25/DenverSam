100+ Craft Breweries in the Mile High City

Denver has more breweries than any other city in America. From massive craft beer destinations to tiny neighborhood taprooms, here's your guide to the best Denver breweries by neighborhood.

Top Denver Breweries

Great Divide Brewing

Denver's Original Craft Pioneer

Since 1994. Two taprooms—downtown and RiNo Barrel Bar. Yeti Imperial Stout is legendary.

$$ Downtown

Ratio Beerworks

RiNo's Favorite Hangout

Massive outdoor space, food trucks, live music. Antidote pale ale is the crowd favorite.

$$ RiNo

Bierstadt Lagerhaus

German Lagers Done Right

Obsessively traditional German-style lagers. The slow-pour pils is worth the wait. Don't skip the pretzel.

$$ Lagers
