Garden of the Gods Climbing

World-Class Sandstone Routes

Garden of the Gods offers some of the most scenic rock climbing in Colorado. Technical sandstone routes with stunning red rock formations. Here's everything you need to know.

More: Boulder Climbing | Colorado Springs Guide

What You Need to Know

  • Free Permit Required: Register at the Visitor Center before climbing
  • Soft Rock: Sandstone requires careful technique—no climbing 24 hours after rain
  • Routes: 300+ routes from 5.2 to 5.13, mostly trad
  • Best Time: Spring and fall; summer mornings work for shaded routes

Classic Routes

North Gateway Rock

Beginner to Intermediate

The most popular formation. Routes from 5.4 to 5.10. Great for first-time Garden climbers.

South Gateway Rock

The Classic Multi-Pitch

The iconic 3-pitch route up the south face. 5.4 with stunning exposure. Iconic photo location.
