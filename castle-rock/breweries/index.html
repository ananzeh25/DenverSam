Castle Rock Breweries

Local craft beer in downtown Castle Rock

Castle Rock has developed a respectable craft beer scene with several local breweries. While it doesn't rival Denver or Fort Collins in volume, the town's breweries offer quality Colorado craft beer in casual, community-oriented settings. Many have become neighborhood gathering spots with reasonable prices compared to Denver.

Castle Rock Breweries

Rockyard Brewing Company

Brewpub - Downtown Castle Rock

One of the area's originals and Castle Rock's flagship brewery. Rockyard brews American and German-style beers in a spacious setting with a full food menu. The outdoor patio is a local favorite during warmer months. Their Lightning Strike Stout and Hopyard IPA are consistently solid. Family-friendly during the day with a more lively bar scene at night.

Must try: Lightning Strike Stout - their award-winner.

Castle Rock Beer Company

Craft Brewery - Downtown

Downtown location with rotating taps in an intimate space focused purely on the beer. The taproom has a neighborhood feel where regulars know the bartenders. They experiment with styles and rotate their lineup frequently, so there's always something new to try. No kitchen, but outside food is welcome and food trucks occasionally park nearby.

Best for: Beer-focused drinkers who appreciate variety.

105 West Brewing Company

Craft Brewery - Off I-25

Named for the highway intersection, 105 West offers a variety of styles in a welcoming taproom. The space is larger than Castle Rock Beer Company with more seating. They brew everything from light lagers to heavy stouts, making it a good choice for groups with different tastes. Regular food truck schedule and occasional live music on weekends.

Good for: Groups with varied beer preferences.

Nearby Brewery Regions

Castle Rock sits between Denver's massive craft beer scene and Colorado Springs. Easy access to both makes it a good base for beer exploration.

Denver Breweries

30 minutes north - 100+ breweries

The craft beer capital of America. RiNo alone has more breweries than most cities. Worth the drive for serious beer enthusiasts.

Colorado Springs Breweries

35 minutes south - Growing scene

Colorado's second-largest city has a growing craft beer scene with several standout breweries worth exploring.

Littleton Breweries

20 minutes north - Suburban gems

Several quality breweries in downtown Littleton's historic district make for a nice half-day beer crawl.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Castle Rock breweries kid-friendly?

Generally yes, especially during afternoon hours. They're community spots, not clubs. Check individual policies for age restrictions in the evening.

Can I walk between breweries?

In downtown, yes. Castle Rock Beer Company is walkable from the historic downtown area. Rockyard and 105 West require driving.

How does it compare to Denver's beer scene?

Much smaller - Denver has hundreds of breweries. But for a day trip or if you're in town, Castle Rock's options are solid and less crowded.

Are there brewery tours?

Not formal tours like larger breweries offer. These are small operations where you can often chat with the brewers at the bar.
