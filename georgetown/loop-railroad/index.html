Georgetown Loop Railroad

A Colorado mountain journey since 1884

The Georgetown Loop is one of Colorado's most iconic train rides—and one of its most impressive engineering feats. Built in 1884 to connect the silver mining towns of Georgetown and Silver Plume, the narrow-gauge railroad climbs 600 feet in just 2 miles, crossing the famous Devil's Gate High Bridge 100 feet above Clear Creek. It was considered an engineering marvel of its time, and riding it today still feels special.

After closing in 1939, the line was restored as a heritage railroad and now operates as one of the most accessible mountain train experiences near Denver—just an hour from downtown.

The Experience

The Train Ride

70-90 minutes round trip

The main experience is a round-trip journey between Georgetown and Silver Plume. You'll board either a steam or diesel locomotive pulling vintage passenger cars through alpine scenery. The highlight is crossing Devil's Gate High Bridge—a curved trestle 300 feet long, 100 feet above the valley floor. Open-air cars offer the best views and photos, though they get cold at altitude.

Tip: Sit on the right side boarding from Georgetown for best bridge views.

Main Experience

Lebanon Mine Tour

Add-on experience

Combine the train ride with a tour of a working 1870s silver mine. At the midpoint of the journey, you disembark and enter the Lebanon Silver Mine with a guide. The tour covers mining history, geology, and what life was like for miners. It's genuinely interesting, not just tourist kitsch. Adds about 45 minutes to the total experience. Book the combo ticket.

Bring: A jacket—the mine stays at 44°F year-round.

Tips for the Best Experience

  • Book ahead for fall colors — September and early October sell out. The aspens along the route are spectacular.
  • Dress in layers — Even summer mornings are cool at 8,500 feet. Open-air cars are colder.
  • Arrive 30 minutes early — Parking, tickets, boarding all take time. Don't cut it close.
  • Explore Georgetown — The Victorian mining town is worth a walk before or after. Good restaurants, historic buildings, and antique shops.
  • Do the mine tour — It adds context and is genuinely interesting. Worth the extra time and cost.
  • Weekdays are less crowded — If you have schedule flexibility, go Tuesday-Thursday.

Special Trains

Santa's North Pole Adventure — Holiday trains with Santa, hot cocoa, and decorations. November through December. Books early—very popular with families.

Oktoberfest Express — Fall trains with beer, brats, and German music. September weekends.

Paranormal Investigation — Night tours for ghost hunters. Yes, really. Limited dates.
