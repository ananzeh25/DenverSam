Georgetown Loop Railroad

Historic Mountain Railway

The Georgetown Loop is one of Colorado's most scenic train rides. This historic narrow-gauge railway crosses a spectacular 95-foot bridge while climbing through the Rocky Mountains. Here's everything you need to know.

More I-70 stops: Idaho Springs | Breckenridge

Quick Facts

  • Duration: 70-minute round trip
  • Season: May through early January
  • Tickets: $35-45 adults, $25-30 kids (book ahead!)
  • Add-on: Lebanon Mine tour available
  • Best seats: Right side going up for best bridge views
