Longmont Breweries

Colorado's secret beer capital

Longmont flies under the radar, and local beer drinkers like it that way. While Denver and Boulder draw the attention, Longmont quietly hosts one of Colorado's densest concentrations of excellent breweries. Left Hand Brewing alone would make the town a destination; add Wibby, Oskar Blues, and a dozen smaller operations, and you have a legitimate beer pilgrimage.

The vibe is less trendy than RiNo, more affordable than Boulder, and genuinely unpretentious. These are breweries built by locals for locals, where the brewing matters more than the branding.

The Anchors

Left Hand Brewing

Boston Avenue

Colorado's nitro pioneers and creators of the legendary Milk Stout. Left Hand has been brewing in Longmont since 1993, and their commitment to quality has never wavered. The taproom pours the full range—from flagships you know to experimental small batches you can only get here. The Nitro Milk Stout on tap is even better than from a can.

Essential: Nitro Milk Stout. Then try the seasonal.

$$ Colorado Legend

Wibby Brewing

Main Street

Lager-focused brewing done exceptionally well. While everyone else chases hazy IPAs, Wibby proves that clean, crisp lagers are just as worthy of craft attention. The Lightshine Helles is perfect; the Volksbier Vienna Lager has won multiple awards. The taproom has a great patio and hosts events. An essential stop for anyone who appreciates the art of lager.

For: People who understand that lagers are hard to make well.

$$ Lager House

Oskar Blues

Main Street (Tasty Weasel)

The pioneers of canned craft beer. Oskar Blues put Dale's Pale Ale in a can before anyone thought it was possible, changing the industry forever. The Tasty Weasel taproom in Longmont pours the full lineup plus rare releases. It's attached to Oskar Blues Grill, so food is covered. A mandatory stop for craft beer history.

History: Where canned craft beer began.

$$ Industry Pioneer

Local Favorites

300 Suns Brewing — Colorado-themed brewing in a friendly taproom. Solid range of styles, good for groups.

Bootstrap Brewing — Self-funded and self-made, Bootstrap brews creative beers in a casual space. The Insane Rush IPA is a local staple.

Grossen Bart Brewery — Neighborhood spot with German influences. The pretzel board is excellent.

Shoes & Brews — Yes, it's a running store and brewery combined. Perfect for Boulder County's endurance crowd.

Brewery Crawl Tips

  • Start on Main Street — Wibby and Oskar Blues are walking distance from each other.
  • Left Hand is a drive — Budget rideshare or a designated driver to include it.
  • Food is available — Most spots have food or trucks; Oskar Blues has a full restaurant.
  • Less crowded than Denver — Weekends are busy but manageable. Weekdays are easy.
  • Combine with Boulder — Longmont is 15 minutes north. Easy to do a Boulder/Longmont crawl.
