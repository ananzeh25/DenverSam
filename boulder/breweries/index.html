Boulder Breweries

Colorado's Craft Beer Capital

Boulder helped pioneer Colorado's craft beer revolution. With more breweries per capita than almost anywhere, this is craft beer heaven. Here are the essential Boulder breweries to visit.

More beer: Denver Breweries | RiNo Breweries | Longmont Breweries

Must-Visit Boulder Breweries

Avery Brewing Company

Big Beers, Bigger Reputation

World-class barrel-aged beers and a stunning taproom with mountain views. Tours available. Don't miss the Maharaja IPA.

$$ Craft Beer

Upslope Brewing

Adventure-Ready Craft Beer

Boulder-born brewery known for outdoor culture and approachable beers. The Craft Lager is perfect post-hike.

$$ Lagers

Mountain Sun Pub

Pearl Street Institution

Cash-only brewpub on Pearl Street. Organic beers, great food, local vibe. A Boulder institution since 1993.

$$ Brewpub
